# Job to Skill


**GitHub Repo** :- https://github.com/sahaavi/Job-to-Skill

**Demo** :- https://youtu.be/rYlRbNha0eM

## Project Description

This project involved scraping job listings from LinkedIn to extract technologies and skills mentioned in the job descriptions. Using GPT-2, we analyzed the job requirements and visualized the distribution of skills required for various positions. Additionally, we generated potential interview questions and answers tailored to the identified skills, aiding in interview preparation and skill development.
