# Introduction to Recommendation System


A recommendation system, also known as a recommender system, is a specialized type of information filtering system rooted in artificial intelligence and machine learning. Leveraging Big Data, these systems analyze diverse criteria such as past purchases, search history, demographic information, and other relevant factors to suggest or recommend additional products or items to consumers. By tailoring suggestions to individual users, recommendation systems play a crucial role in helping users explore and discover products and services they might not have encountered independently.

Types of Recommendation System:
1. Popularity based recommender system
2. Classification
3. COntent based recommender systems
4. Collaborative filtering
5. Hybrid approaches
